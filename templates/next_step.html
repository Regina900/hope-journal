A small step can matter

Your Next Step 🌱

You don't have to solve everything today. When life feels heavy, choosing one small, practical action can help you move through the moment gently.

🌱

Start With One Small Step

You don't need to do everything at once. Choose what feels manageable right now.

Try this now

Reach out to someone you trust 🤝

You don't have to explain everything. You could simply send a message such as, “I'm having a difficult day. Can we talk?” Letting someone know how you're doing can make a difficult moment feel less lonely.

🌬️

Pause and breathe

Take five slow breaths and give yourself a quiet moment.

💧

Care for your body

Drink some water, stretch, or step away from your screen for a few minutes.

✍️

Put it into words

Write down one thing you're feeling without worrying about making it perfect.

💜 When things feel unsafe

You don't have to handle a crisis alone. Consider staying with someone you trust, telling them how you're feeling, and seeking immediate local emergency or crisis support if you believe you may hurt yourself.
